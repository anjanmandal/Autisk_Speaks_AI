The Challenge – Autism Across the Lifespan

👶

Early Communication

  • 1 in 36 US 8‑y‑olds diagnosed (CDC).
  • 70 % preschoolers show atypical language.
  • Classroom talk & play become hard.
🎧

Sensory Processing

  • 45–95 % show sensory hyper/hypo‑reactivity.
  • Triggers unpredictable meltdowns.
  • Families live on constant alert.
🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Teen Social‑Emotional

  • ≈40 % experience anxiety.
  • Need weekly guided practice (PEERS®).
  • Few stigma‑free spaces to rehearse.
🙂

Emotion Recognition

  • Difficulties reading faces & tone.
  • Leads to misunderstandings.
  • Amplifies exclusion & stress.
💼

Adult Services Gap

  • 50–80 % unemployment.
  • Many counties lack adult clinics.
  • Parents remain default carers.
👨‍👩‍👧

Family Burden

  • PIIs work when parents get feedback.
  • No single dashboard exists today.
  • Data silos add stress.
